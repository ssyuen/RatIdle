Privacy policy

RatIdle · last updated 24 October 2026

RatIdle is a cosy idle game made by Samuel Yuen. This page explains what data the game uses and why. In short: your game lives on your device, there are no ads and no tracking, and the only things that ever leave your device are an optional cloud save and, in the app, the record of a purchase.

Data kept on your device

Your save slots, game log and settings are stored on your device (in the browser, or in the app's storage). They never leave it unless you export a save yourself or turn on cloud saves.

Cloud saves (optional)

If you choose to set up cloud saves, you create an account with an email address and a password. We use Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to store:

  • your email address and a verified/unverified flag, to sign you in and verify the address;
  • a copy of each save slot you back up.

Only you can read or write your saves. Turning cloud saves off lets you delete every cloud copy and your account from inside the game (Options › Cloud save). Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

RatIdle Pro is a one-time purchase. In the iOS app, payment is handled by Apple; we never see your card or Apple ID. On the website, payment is processed by Stripe, which collects your card details, email and billing country to take the payment, prevent fraud and send your receipt; we never see your full card number. Stripe's privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy. We use RevenueCat to check whether you own RatIdle Pro. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID it creates, your purchase receipt and transaction history for this app, and basic device information (such as the app version and operating system). It is not linked to your name or email. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Notifications

The app can schedule a local notification for when a focus timer ends. It is scheduled on your device and nothing is sent to a server.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no ad or analytics SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

Children

RatIdle is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Cloud saves are optional and need an email address; a parent can delete a child's account at any time from inside the game.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions or deletion requests: email samuel.s.yuen@gmail.com, or open an issue at github.com/ssyuen/RatIdle/issues.
